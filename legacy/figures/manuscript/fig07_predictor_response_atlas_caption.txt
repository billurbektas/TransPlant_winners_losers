Predicted change in area under the relative-cover curve (Δ AUC; warmed minus origin-control) across
destination summer climate. Predictions are population-level fixed-effect values from the current
full models. Low and High continuous traits are the 25th and 75th percentiles across unique
trait-complete species: LDMC 0.1958 and 0.3294 g/g; LMA 0.04247 and 0.07229 1/(mm² mg⁻¹); and
vegetative height 0.0611 and 0.2820 m. Low and High warming are Q25 and Q75 across 42 distinct
experiments (ΔT = 1.51 and 3.36 °C). Models remain continuous; Low and High values are
representative prediction contrasts rather than fitted categories. Points show observed experiment
climates, and coloured areas are restricted to observed three-dimensional
temperature–precipitation–warming support; support is calculated separately for Non-woody and Woody
species. PEV1 is shown as a representative phylogenetic gradient (Q25 = -59.582; Q75 = 11.421)
while PEV2 and PEV3 are held at their standardized reference values of zero. Phylogenetic inference
is based on the joint PEV1–PEV3 model, and the sign of PEV1 is not a universal biological
direction. All panels share one zero-centred colour scale because the supported Woodiness range did
not materially exceed the other predictor ranges.
